A space to think aloud.

The Princeton Society of Philosophy Students is dedicated to fostering a space on campus where anyone curious about philosophy can engage in meaningful discussions and learn from contemporary thinkers through guest talks and events. We hope to build a community where everyone feels welcome to think aloud, ask big questions, and grow through conversation.

This week's big question What do we owe each other?

What we do

Two things, every other week

No background in philosophy needed for either one — just curiosity.

Biweekly

Guest speakers & discussions

Talks from contemporary thinkers, followed by open group discussion. Come with questions, leave with more of them.

Biweekly

Reading group

A smaller group working through texts together for deeper study — slowly, carefully, and out loud.
